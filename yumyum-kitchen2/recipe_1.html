Ingredients:

Serves: 6

  • 8 ounces rice noodles or spaghetti
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey or brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon sriracha or chili garlic sauce
  • 1/4 cup warm water (to thin the sauce)
  • 1/4 cup shredded carrots
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onions
  • 1 tablespoon chopped peanuts for garnish

Spicy Thai Peanut Noodles Cooking Instructions

  1. Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, sriracha, and warm water until smooth.
  5. Add the cooked noodles to the skillet and pour the peanut sauce over them.
  6. Toss the noodles gently until they are evenly coated with the sauce.
  7. Add the shredded carrots and mix them into the noodles.
  8. Cook for another 1–2 minutes until everything is heated through.
  9. Remove from heat and top with chopped green onions and peanuts.
  10. Serve warm and enjoy!
