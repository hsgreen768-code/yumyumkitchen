Ingredients:

Serves: 6

  • 2 slices of whole grain or sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar (for poaching)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Fresh lemon juice (optional)
  • Fresh herbs such as parsley or chives for garnish

Avocado Toast with Poached Egg Cooking Instructions

  1. Fill a medium saucepan with water and bring it to a gentle simmer. Add the white vinegar.
  2. Crack one egg into a small bowl. Stir the simmering water gently to create a small whirlpool.
  3. Carefully slide the egg into the center of the water and let it cook for about 3 minutes until the whites are set but the yolk remains soft.
  4. Remove the egg with a slotted spoon and place it on a paper towel. Repeat with the second egg.
  5. While the eggs are cooking, toast the bread slices until golden and crisp.
  6. Mash the avocado in a small bowl and mix with a pinch of salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  7. Spread the mashed avocado evenly over each slice of toast.
  8. Place one poached egg on top of each slice of avocado toast.
  9. Season with additional salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if desired.
  10. Garnish with fresh herbs and serve immediately.
